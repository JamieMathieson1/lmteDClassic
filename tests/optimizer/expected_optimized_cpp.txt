EXPECTED OPTIMISED C++ (illustrative semantic output)

The exact whitespace, includes, and function formatting depend on the project's
CppGenerator implementation. The integration runner writes the actual output to
 tests/generated_cpp.txt.

Expected optimisation effects:
  - (5 + 3) * 2       becomes 16
  - 100 \ (2 + 3)     becomes 20 (integer division)
  - 7 * 6 - 8         becomes 34
  - 5 > 3             selects the true branch
  - V_int_unused      is removed if its declaration is marked isDecl in the AST
  - condition loop    is expanded into three copies of its body and step

    i = i + 1;
    sum = sum + i;
    i = i + 1;

    result = result + 4;
